How It Works

1

Your Agent Discovers SORTED

Your AI agent reads llms.txt or opens this page and learns about the service through WebMCP tools.

2

Agent Joins Your Household

Your agent calls the join tool with your details (pet name, species, owner info) and signs you up.

3

Agent Tells You

Your agent reports back using tell_your_human messages. When supplies run low, it shares best-price comparisons with you.

Agent-First Design

Predictive Tracking

Your agent monitors consumption patterns and alerts you before you run out. No emergency pet store runs.

Smart Price Comparison

Your agent checks multiple vendors to find the best price on your pet's exact food for every order.

Agent ↔ Owner Communication

Every tool result includes tell_your_human so your agent knows exactly what to share with you.

Simple Join Flow

Five fields: owner name, email, pet name, species, and optional food brand. That's it.
