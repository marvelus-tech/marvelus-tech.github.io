How it works

  1. 01

    Transcribe

    Local Whisper, AssemblyAI, or Deepgram.

  2. 02

    Claims

    Extract and type claims, then group them into topics.

  3. 03

    Citations

    Research checkable claims and keep facts with real source URLs.

  4. 04

    Topic pages

    Scrollable pages and a timeline for replay or live listening.
