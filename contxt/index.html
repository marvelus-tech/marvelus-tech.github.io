CONTXT

A live context companion for anything spoken. Transcription becomes claims, research attaches citations, and you browse topic pages instead of a flat transcript.

Play debate demo Open sample deck Run on GitHub

How it works

  1. Transcribe audio (local Whisper, AssemblyAI, or Deepgram).
  2. Extract and type claims; group them into topics.
  3. Research checkable claims and keep facts with real source URLs.
  4. Build scrollable topic pages and a timeline you can navigate during replay or live ingestion.

What you can try today

  • Play demo: scrub real debate audio and watch pages appear (/contxt/play/).
  • Sample deck: short fixture replay with cited facts (no API keys).
  • Your machine: npm run ci:replay in the repo.
  • Debate bakeoff: see transcription results on a 1992 clip.
