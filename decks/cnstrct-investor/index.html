Investor overview · Nov 2026

CNSTRCT

Your AI co-founder for businesses you can start with almost no capital

We triage founders, surface three vetted opportunities, spin up a customized site and operating system, and deploy proactive agents as founding teammates.

Problem

Americans file millions of business applications, then stall before a repeatable customer motion

Formation is cheap; operating is not. Solo founders still need positioning, a credible web presence, lead capture, partner outreach, and daily execution without hiring a team on day one.

5.7M U.S. business applications in calendar 2025 (seasonally adjusted monthly series, Census BFS).
~524K Monthly pace in the first ten months of 2026, still above pre-2020 norms.

Why now

Solopreneurs try AI before they hire, and agent platforms are crossing into production budgets

The cultural shift is "co-founder in software," not another chatbot. Enterprise agent spend is forecast to scale fast, while the smallest businesses already route capacity to AI first.

86% Solopreneurs and microbusiness owners who try AI before hiring for tasks they cannot handle alone (FreshBooks / Wakefield, 2026).
+26.8% Rise in no-early-hire business applications in AI-exposed industries, Q1 2024 to Q1 2026 (Mercatus / CNBC analysis).
Forecast Enterprise agentic AI software spend from ~$1.5B (2025) toward ~$41.8B (2030), Omdia; ranges vary by definition.

Solution

CNSTRCT turns intent into an operating business in one guided session

1 · Triage5-8 conversational questions on skills, capital, location, and appetite for trades/home services.
2 · ChooseThree opportunity cards: two marked coming soon, one live path personalized to the profile.
3 · LaunchGenerated marketing site plus owner dashboard: agents, approvals, and computer/sandbox view.

Output is a working micro-business, not a blank repo. Former internal codename: Kindling. Product name everywhere: CNSTRCT.

Demo

Home-services lead arbitrage is our live MVP: site, agents, and human-in-the-loop outreach

  • Generated site at a shareable path: hero, how-it-works, homeowner lead form, about, contact, dashboard link.
  • Agent A finds local tradies and drafts partnership outreach (search and directories).
  • Agent B captures or matches homeowner leads; second agent may ship as coming-soon if schedule is tight.
  • Approval gate on every external send, post, or spend; demo runs with mocked sends when API keys are absent.

Market

Our wedge is the solo and micro-business formation wave, not generic enterprise automation

Top of funnel: U.S. business applications at record levels. SAM: capital-constrained founders who want a packaged playbook plus agents, starting with local services arbitrage and adjacent low-CAPEX models.

TAM (forecast) AI agents market estimates from ~$7.8B (2025) toward ~$52B (2030), 46% CAGR, MarketsandMarkets. Treat as third-party forecast.
58% Small businesses using generative AI in 2025 vs 23% two years prior (U.S. Chamber survey, cited in trade press).

Business model

Subscription for the business OS, plus metered agent compute and LLM usage

  • Core plan includes triage, one active business template, hosted site path, dashboard, and starter agent routines.
  • Usage for proactive agent hours, computer/sandbox time, and outbound actions once approvals are enabled.
  • Expansion as we unlock additional opportunity cards (same OS, new vertical playbooks).
  • Status pricing and packaging in design; not yet in market.

Traction

MVP is built and demoable end-to-end without production API keys

  • Product conversational triage, three-card selector, home-services template, generated site, agent chat, sandbox/computer view, approval UX.
  • Revenue none to date; no invented user or MRR figures.
  • Pilots internal demos; seeking design partners among first-time founders and micro-agency operators.
  • Stack Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, multi-tenant shape, optional Supabase auth, Orgo or local sandbox for agent computers.

Go-to-market

We meet founders where ambition outruns capital: content, communities, and local arbitrage stories

  • Founder-led narrative documenting "zero to first lead" builds with CNSTRCT (proof over promises).
  • Vertical landing pages per opportunity card; home services first, next templates from the same engine.
  • Partners formation-adjacent educators and local trade networks for supply-side liquidity.
  • Product-led loop shareable generated sites as organic distribution for homeowner demand.

Risks

We design for agent cost, trust, and vertical liquidity up front

  • Unit economics proactive agents can incur meaningful compute; usage pricing and routine efficiency matter.
  • Trust outbound actions require approvals, audit logs, and clear mock-vs-live behavior in demos.
  • Vertical risk home services needs both homeowner demand and tradie supply in each geo.
  • Platform dependency LLM, sandbox, and email providers; we keep a no-key demo path for evaluation.
